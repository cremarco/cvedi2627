Use case: productivity-visual.
Asset type: transparent illustration atlas for a university Slidev teaching slide about the UX design process, coordinated with an editorial booklet whose visual motif is colored metro routes.
Create ONE landscape canvas containing exactly FIVE separate, beautifully crafted isometric miniature paper-and-model scenes, all at the same scale, in a single horizontal row. This is an illustration asset, NOT a finished slide or infographic. No text, no letters, no numbers, no labels, no logos.
The five scenes, left to right:
1. Setting up a project: a small standing presentation board with neatly arranged blank sticky notes, a blank brief sheet, and a precise pencil.
2. Understanding people: a large magnifying glass over two abstract paper profile cards and a small pair of blank conversation bubbles.
3. Organizing a journey: blank connected task cards laid out as a compact branching journey map with thin rails and round junctions.
4. Structuring information: stacked white paper wireframe sheets and a small architectural grid of interface rectangles, suggesting a carefully built structure.
5. Testing and refining: a miniature browser window with blank interface shapes, a large teal cursor, and a warm orange adjustment pencil.
Style: sophisticated editorial 3D papercraft and architectural scale model rendering, crisp folded cardstock, elegant precise details, softly beveled layers and convincing subtle material depth. Restrained, adult, design-school quality. Not flat icons, not emoji, not childish cartoon toys. Objects must be recognizable at small scale.
Palette: deep petrol teal #005f5a and teal #00786f, vivid warm orange #ff6900, warm pale orange #ffd6a7, white paper. Absolutely no purple, pink or blue, no rainbow.
Composition is critical: exactly five equal-width invisible vertical columns. Centers of the scenes at x=10%,30%,50%,70%,90% and y=50%. Each entire scene fits comfortably in its own column with generous clear transparent gaps, and is contained within the central horizontal 26% band of canvas height. No scene overlaps another. Above and below that central band is transparent empty space. Keep all object edges within the canvas. Scenes should be wide compact arrangements, never tall towers.
Background: genuine transparent alpha. No background rectangle, no floor plane, no checkerboard baked in, no opaque white or colored background, no decorative frame. No connecting line between scenes; route graphics and all typography will be added in code.
